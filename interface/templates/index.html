Neural Network Chain

Our system uses two stacked MLP models to capture complex nonlinear patterns in patient vitals, producing highly accurate baseline risk probabilities.

Expert Rules (CF)

A Certainty Factor (CF) ruleset incorporates clinical domain knowledge, adjusting the neural output based on high-impact physiological triggers.

💬

Explainable AI

Integrated with Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct to provide real-time, grounded explanations of why a specific risk level was assigned to a patient.

Built for Transparency

This project implements a Hybrid Expert System. While pure deep learning models often act as "black boxes," our hybrid approach ensures every diagnosis has a clear Chain of Evidence.

The system was trained on a dataset of 1,014 patients from rural health centers in Bangladesh, considering features such as Age, Blood Pressure, Blood Sugar, Body Temperature, and Heart Rate. By combining data-driven insights with clinical logic, we provide a tool that is both powerful and explainable.
